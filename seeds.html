zms301

This three-way cross hybrid matures in 90-95 days and yields 5-6mt/ha. It features extra early maturity, drought tolerance, stay-green traits, and is suitable for green maize. It is lodging tolerant, widely adaptable, and resistant to diseases like cob rot, GLS, MSV, leaf blight, and rust..

zms301

This very early maturity, three-way cross hybrid features dent-like grain, matures in 95-100 days, and yields 9mt/ha. It has long, fully husked cobs, a long shank for quick cob drop, and good disease tolerance to cob rot, GLS, MSV, leaf blight, and rust..

zms608

This medium-maturity three-way cross yellow hybrid matures in 115-120 days, yielding 10-12mt/ha. It offers moderate drought tolerance, good lodging resistance, and robust disease tolerance. Ideal for both stock feed and green maize, it features double cobbing, wider adaptation across all three agro-ecological zones (I, II, III)..

Digoronto Agric-Shop was founded with a single, clear purpose to make quality agricultural inputs accessible to every farmer in Botswana, whether they till one hectare or one hundred. The name "Digoronto" — meaning fruits in Setswana — reflects our inspiration from the desire to symbolize the ultimate results of a harvest and the hardworking farmers who feed our communities. Starting from our flagship branch in Moshupa, we have grown steadily by listening to farmers, understanding their unique challenges and stocking solutions that actually work in Botswana's climate and soils.
